Privacy Policy for Cymatics

Last updated: June 05, 2026

Tzion Hanses, creator of Cymatics ("I," "me," or "my"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how I handle your information when you use my website at ezrafrey-2006.github.io.

Important Note: I do not store your personal data or YouTube information on any external servers. All data is processed locally in your browser and is gone once you close the session.

1. Information I Access

When you log in using your Google Account to access YouTube features, I temporarily access:

  • Your Google email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture).
  • Your YouTube account data (such as playlists or liked videos) specifically to allow you to play music within the app.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

My application uses YouTube API Services to provide music playback and reactivity features. By using my application, you are also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

I use this data only to display and play your music. I do not store your YouTube data; it is processed locally in your browser.

3. How I Use Your Information

I use the accessed information solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide access to your YouTube content.
  • Enable the music reactivity (Cymatics) features of the app.
  • Save your app preferences locally in your browser (using LocalStorage).

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

I do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or YouTube data with any third parties. Your data is used only within the Cymatics application interface.

5. Data Security and Deletion

I do not store your personal data on any external servers. All authentication is handled securely via Google OAuth. You can revoke my app's access to your data at any time via your Google Security Settings.

To request deletion of any locally saved preferences, please contact me at: tzionhanses1@gmail.com.

For more information on how Google handles your data, please visit the Google Privacy Policy.
